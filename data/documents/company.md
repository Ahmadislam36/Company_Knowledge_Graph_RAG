# Nexora Technologies

Nexora Technologies is a software and artificial intelligence company based in Lahore, Pakistan. The company provides software development, artificial intelligence, data analytics, and cloud solutions to businesses.

Nexora Technologies has four major departments:

1. Artificial Intelligence Department
2. Software Engineering Department
3. Data Science Department
4. Cloud and DevOps Department

The Artificial Intelligence Department develops AI-powered applications, retrieval-augmented generation systems, and multi-agent systems.

The Software Engineering Department develops web applications, backend APIs, and enterprise software.

The Data Science Department works on data analytics, machine learning models, customer analytics, and predictive systems.

The Cloud and DevOps Department manages cloud infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, containers, monitoring, and production deployments.

The company follows a project-based development model. Projects are assigned to departments and managed by project managers. Employees can work on multiple projects depending on their responsibilities.
